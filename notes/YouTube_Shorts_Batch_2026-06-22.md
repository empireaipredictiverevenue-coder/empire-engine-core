## Production Notes

- **Format**: All Shorts in 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30-45s duration
- **Voiceover**: Real voice (ElevenLabs or human). NO AI TTS — it sounds
  wrong to contractors. The brain can suggest tone but not voice.
- **Captions**: Subtitles required (85% of mobile viewers watch muted)
- **B-roll**: Mix of stock footage (storm clouds, roofs, contractors
  working) + screen recordings (BBB scrape, calculator, map)
- **Branding**: Empire AI logo in bottom-right corner, last 5s of every Short
- **End card**: 2s static with "/for-contractors" + subscribe button
