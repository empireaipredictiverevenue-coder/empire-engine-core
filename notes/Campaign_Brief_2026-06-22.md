## Brand DNA Summary

Empire AI is a vendor-mode B2B platform for storm-damage and restoration
contractors. Voice is direct, slightly irreverent, operator-to-operator.
Targets working contractors who'd rather have a phone call than a
whitepaper. Pricing is mid-tier ($99-499/mo) with crypto-native payments
(no Stripe required).

## Campaign Concepts

### Concept 1: "The 24-Hour Delay"
**Hypothesis:** Roofers are losing jobs because Free-tier competitors see
the lead 24 hours after they do. Pain leads.
**Primary Message:** "Right now another roofer is on your lead. He's on
Free. You're on Basic. Difference: 24 hours."
**Tone:** Operator-direct, mild jealousy-trigger
**Visual Direction:** Split image: clock showing 24:00 vs clock showing
0:00. Roofing site in background. Slight urgency.
**Target Platforms:** Email subject line + 1:1 voice call
**CTA:** "Get the 24-hour head start. $99/mo Basic."
**Addresses:** Lead-delay pain point
**Framework:** PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve)

### Concept 2: "The Vendor Math"
**Hypothesis:** Contractors want to know: how much does the lead cost,
how much do I keep? Frame as unit economics.
**Primary Message:** "$99/mo / 50 leads = $1.98/lead. Close 5% = 2.5 jobs/mo
at $4,200 each = $10,500 gross. Tier cost is rounding error."
**Tone:** Specific, math-driven, peer-to-peer
**Visual Direction:** Simple ROI calc, no stock photos. Maybe a
calculator screenshot. Bold numbers.
**Target Platforms:** Email body + landing page hero variant
**CTA:** "See the math. Activate Pro."
**Addresses:** Price sensitivity, ROI justification
**Framework:** 4P (Promise, Picture, Proof, Push)

### Concept 3: "What the Stripe-Free Contractor Looks Like"
**Hypothesis:** A meaningful chunk of contractors can't pass Stripe KYC.
Empire doesn't care — USDC only. Turn a constraint into a differentiator.
**Primary Message:** "No card on file. No KYC. No Stripe. Just USDC to a
wallet. Same-day activate."
**Tone:** Slightly rebellious, anti-establishment
**Visual Direction:** "No Stripe" with a slash through it. USDC logo. Bold.
**Target Platforms:** Email + landing page second variant
**CTA:** "Activate with USDC. Instant."
**Addresses:** Stripe-rejection pool (real segment)
**Framework:** Star-Story-Solution (rare tribe / inside-the-fence)
