## Pricing Model Comparison

| Model | Asset Sold | CPL Range | Conv Rate | Best For |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Pay-Per-Lead (PPL)** | Form fill (name, email, phone) | $2–$1,000+ | 5–15% | High volume, nurturing cycles, CRM entry |
| **Pay-Per-Call (PPCall)** | Live inbound conversation | $10–$400+ | 20–40% | Urgent needs, complex decisions, high-ticket |

**Key insight:** The most successful agencies run both models simultaneously — PPL for top-of-funnel volume, PPCall for bottom-of-funnel conversion.

## PPL vs PPC: Decision Matrix

| If your vertical is... | Use PPL | Use PPC |
|------------------------|---------|---------|
| Emergency/urgent need | | ✅ (burst pipe, storm damage) |
| Long consideration cycle | ✅ (solar, mortgage) | |
| High-ticket transaction | ✅ (legal, B2B) | ✅ (insurance, legal) |
| Recurring revenue model | ✅ (pest control, home security) | ✅ (senior care) |
| Complex decision | ✅ (Medicare, debt settlement) | ✅ (Medicare, addiction) |
| High volume needed | ✅ (auto insurance, auto repair) | |
