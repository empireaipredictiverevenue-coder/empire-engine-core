## Fundamental Analysis Drivers
### Central Bank Policy
- **Fed (USD)**: FOMC meetings, rate decisions, dot plot, QT/QE
- **ECB (EUR)**: Interest rate, PEPP/APP, Lagarde speeches
- **BOE (GBP)**: Bank Rate, MPC votes, Bailey guidance
- **BOJ (JPY)**: Interest rate, YCC policy, Ueda commentary
- **RBA (AUD)**: Cash rate, Bullock statements, employment focus
- **RBNZ (NZD)**: OCR, Orr guidance, dairy prices
- **BOC (CAD)**: Overnight rate, Macklem, oil prices

### Key Economic Indicators
- **Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)**: 1st Friday of month — biggest market mover
- **CPI (Inflation)**: Monthly — directly impacts rate decisions
- **GDP**: Quarterly — overall economic health
- **PMI**: Manufacturing & Services — leading indicator
- **Retail Sales**: Monthly — consumer spending health
- **Unemployment Rate**: Labor market strength
- **Industrial Production**: Manufacturing output
- **Consumer Confidence**: Sentiment leading indicator

## Position Sizing
```
Position Size (lots) = (Account Risk $) / (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value)

Example:
  Account: $10,000
  Risk: 1% = $100
  Stop: 50 pips
  EUR/USD pip value for 1 standard lot = $10
  Position = $100 / (50 × $10) = 0.2 lots

Lot Sizes:
  Standard lot = 100,000 units ($10/pip for EUR/USD)
  Mini lot = 10,000 units ($1/pip)
  Micro lot = 1,000 units ($0.10/pip)
  Nano lot = 100 units ($0.01/pip)
```

## Risk Parameters
- Max leverage: depends on broker (30:1 for retail forex, unlimited for professional)
- Max position size: 2% risk per trade max
- Daily loss limit: 5% of account (stop trading for the day)
- Correlation limit: don't trade highly correlated pairs simultaneously
- News avoidance: avoid trading 30 min before/after major economic releases

## Key Technical Levels for Forex
- **Psychological levels**: round numbers (1.1000, 1.2000, etc.)
- **Pivot points**: calculated from previous day's high/low/close
- **S/R from previous week/month**: major support/resistance
- **Order blocks**: institutional supply/demand zones
- **Liquidity sweeps**: price going above recent high to trigger stops, then reversing
