# Channel Optimization Reference

## Channel Mix Strategy
- **PPC**: Best for immediate results. High cost, fast leads. Cap at 40% of budget.
- **SEO**: Longest ROI timeline (3-6 months). Lowest CPL. Essential for sustainability.
- **Native Ads**: Good for brand awareness. Moderate CPL. Test 5+ creatives per campaign.
- **Email/SMS**: Highest reply rate when targeted. Requires clean lists and compliance.
- **Social**: Best for organic reach. Low cost, high effort for engagement.
- **Affiliates**: Performance-based. Pay only for results. Scale with proven partners.

## CPL Benchmarks
| Channel | Target CPL | Max CPL | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPC | $12 | $20 | 30-40% |
| SEO | $4 | $8 | 15-25% |
| Native | $10 | $18 | 10-15% |
| Email | $3 | $6 | 10-15% |
| Social | $6 | $12 | 5-10% |
| Affiliates | $8 | $15 | 5-10% |

## Optimization Rules
- Kill campaigns exceeding max CPL for 7+ days
- Double down on channels with < 70% of target CPL
- No single channel exceeds 50% of total budget
- Rotate creative every 14 days per channel
