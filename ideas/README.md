## How to add an idea

One line, ~80 chars, no detail. Detail goes in the project doc, not here.
Bad: "build predictive revenue ecosystem with AGI/SI on top of empire-v49
using reinforcement learning, sentiment analysis, and a multi-agent swarm"
Good: "predictive-revenue layer on top of empire-v49"

Format: `- [YYYY-MM-DD] one-line idea`

## KEEP criteria (the STARTING_POINT.md filter)

A kept idea must answer YES to ALL THREE:
1. **Does it serve the storm-damage lead-gen business directly?**
   (Not "AI for AI's sake", not "future empire", not "platform play".)
2. **Does it produce a real, measurable outcome?**
   (Not "improved learning", not "smarter decisions" — an outcome
   you could point to in a sentence: "1 more lead", "1 fewer
   complaint", "$X revenue".)
3. **Does it have at least 1 real data point to learn from?**
   (Not zero. Not stub data. Not "we'll know once it runs".)

If any answer is NO → DEFER or DROP.

## DEFER vs DROP

DEFER = interesting but blocked on a real outcome we don't have yet.
         (Re-check when the blocking outcome exists.)
DROP  = doesn't serve the business, or violates the spirit of
        STARTING_POINT.md ("AI agents that build other AI agents",
        "sovereign omni-intelligence", "physics equations", etc.)

## Format

```
- [YYYY-MM-DD] one-line idea · ~why
```

`~why` is optional, single phrase. Not a paragraph.
