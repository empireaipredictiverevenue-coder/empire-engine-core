# Target Goal
Optimize cold email subject lines to maximize the open rate metric.

# Asset To Mutate
You are only allowed to modify the 'subjects.json' file. Do not touch any other file in this directory.

# Psychological Copywriting Rules
1. Use the power of suggestion. Keep it casual, low-pressure, and highly relevant.
2. Maximize curiosity. Never sound like a marketing blast.
3. Lowercase letters work best. Write like a regular person sending a quick note to a peer.
4. Keep length under 5 words. Short lines get cut off less on mobile screens.
5. No emojis, no buzzwords, and no fake 'RE:' text.

# Execution Strategy
Analyze the historical open rates inside 'subjects.json'. Look at what failed and what won. Generate one new highly optimized mutation. Overwrite the file with your new variant for the next batch test.
