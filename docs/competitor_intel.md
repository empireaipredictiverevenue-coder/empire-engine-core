# Competitor Intelligence — Empire AI

## ZoomInfo
- **Pricing:** $15k–$60k+/yr annual contract (custom quote, opaque)
- **Strengths:** 200M+ B2B profiles, deep firmographics, intent data
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Prohibitively expensive for SMB contractors ($15k minimum)
  - Long-term rigid contracts — hard to cancel
  - Data decay ~30% annually (outdated contacts)
  - No storm/insurance niche specialization
  - No real-time property event triggers
  - Requires dedicated SDR team to use effectively
- **Best for:** Enterprise companies with large sales teams

## Apollo.io
- **Pricing:** Free tier (10k emails/mo), $49/mo Basic, $99/mo Pro, $119/mo Organization
- **Strengths:** Affordable entry point, good free tier, buyer intent signals, integrated sequences
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Credit system for phone/email exports → unpredictable overage costs
  - Inconsistent data quality for local contractor niches
  - No event-driven lead generation
  - No construction/project-specific timing data
  - Designed for full-time SDRs, not small business owners
- **Best for:** Email-first outbound teams on a budget

## Empire AI Superpowers (vs all competitors)

1. **Storm-event triggers** — real-time property damage detection (nobody else does this)
2. **Niche specialization** — roofing, restoration, public adjusters, commercial contractors
3. **No upfront cost** — 3% success fee on settled claims only
4. **First 2 deals free** — zero-risk trial for contractors
5. **SMS-first outreach** — higher reply rates than cold email (our data: ~8-12%)
6. **Automated dispatch** — contractor matched within minutes of YES reply
7. **TCPA-compliant pipeline** — reduces legal risk for contractors
8. **Free self-onboarding** — no sales call required, 90-second signup
