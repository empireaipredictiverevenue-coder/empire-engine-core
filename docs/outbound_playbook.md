# EMPIRE AI — OUTBOUND CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK
### Lead Generation Architecture — Grade 6 Reading Level

## NICHE 1: COMMERCIAL ROOFING

### Pattern-Breaker Hook
> "Your roof leaked through **$14,000 in inventory** last night and you won't know it until your morning walkthrough."

**Why it works:** Commercial owners don't check the roof. They check the P&L. This hook bridges the gap between "invisible damage" and "visible money loss." It lands in their world — the warehouse floor, not the rooftop.

**Delivery channels:** SMS (first 30 characters hit the preview), email subject line, cold call opener.

---

### Micro Lead Magnet Asset
**"The 72-Hour Storm Window Report"** — a 1-page PDF showing:
- The **exact date and time** the last hail/wind event hit their ZIP code
- **3 photos** of neighboring commercial roofs with circled damage (pulled from satellite + drone)
- A single line at the bottom: *"Properties inspected within 72 hours of a storm event receive FULL insurance claim processing. After 72 hours, adjusters begin flagging pre-existing condition exclusions."*

**Delivery method:** Text them a link. No email gate. No form. "Tap here for your property's storm window — it closes in 18 hours."

---

### Performance Pitch
> "This works like a **grocery store sampling program**. We don't ask you to buy the cereal. We just hand you the spoon. A property owner requests an inspection. We connect them to YOUR phone. You pay ONLY when that phone rings with a real human on the other end. No ring, no bill. Same way Frito-Lay doesn't charge the grocery store for samples nobody takes."

**Follow-up line:** "You're not buying ads. You're buying RINGING PHONES."

## NICHE 2: COMMERCIAL SOLAR

### Pattern-Breaker Hook
> "Your electric meter is charging you **$2,800 more this year** than the identical warehouse next door — and the only difference is they installed panels in 2023."

**Why it works:** Comparison is the most primal human decision shortcut. When you tell a business owner their competitor is getting something cheaper, you don't need to sell solar. You just need to sell fairness.

**Delivery channels:** LinkedIn DM, direct mail postcard (side-by-side building photos), cold email.

---

### Micro Lead Magnet Asset
**"The Kilowatt Neighbor Report"** — a 1-page data sheet showing:
- Their building's estimated annual electric cost (pulled from public sq footage × local utility rates)
- **3 nearby commercial addresses** in their ZIP with confirmed solar installs (public permit data)
- Estimated savings gap: *"Your building vs. the solar buildings on your block — ${X} per year"*
- No solar jargon. Just: "Here's what you're paying. Here's what they're paying. Want to fix the gap?"

**Delivery method:** Text or email the PDF. First line: "I ran your address against the county permit database. Your neighbors are paying less than you."

---

### Performance Pitch
> "Imagine you're boarding a **plane** and the gate agent says: 'We overbooked. We'll pay $500 to anyone who takes the next flight.' The airline doesn't pay for volunteers they don't get. They pay ONLY when a hand goes up. That's our model. A business owner raises their hand for a solar assessment. We route them to YOU. You pay ONLY for that hand-raise. No ads. No clicks. No 'impressions.' Just raised hands and ringing phones."

**Follow-up line:** "We only get paid when you get a live transfer. If the lead doesn't pick up? You don't pay. If they hang up in 8 seconds? You don't pay. Simple as that."

## NICHE 3: DEBT RELIEF

### Pattern-Breaker Hook
> "Your credit score dropped **47 points last month** and you don't know it yet because you're afraid to check."

**Why it works:** Debt relief prospects live in avoidance. They know the problem. They're not opening the mail. They're not checking Credit Karma. This hook names the behavior they're already doing (avoiding) and quantifies the pain they're already feeling (numbers feel real even if they're not exact).

**Delivery channels:** Facebook/Instagram ad creative (text overlay on dark background), YouTube pre-roll, SMS to aged debt leads.

---

### Micro Lead Magnet Asset
**"The 90-Second Creditor Scorecard"** — an instant interactive tool:
- Prospect enters **3 numbers**: total owed, monthly minimums, and rough income
- Tool spits out a single page with their **"Debt-to-Freedom Ratio"** (how many months until payoff at current pace vs. with negotiation)
- No forms. No "call us." Just: *"At your current rate: 11 years, 4 months. With negotiation: 3 years, 2 months. Want to see how?"*
- The gap IS the pitch.

**Delivery method:** Link in text or ad. Loads in browser. No email required. The tool itself is the opt-in.

---

### Performance Pitch
> "Picture a **grocery checkout lane**. You put items on the belt. The cashier rings them up. You pay ONLY for what's in your cart. You don't pay for the guy behind you. You don't pay a 'maybe someday' fee. Our model is the same checkout lane. A person with debt raises their hand. They get connected to YOUR team. You pay ONLY when that live connection happens. One cart. One call. One bill. No mystery charges."

**Follow-up line:** "You're not buying a 'lead list.' You're buying a ringing phone with a qualified debtor who just asked for help."

## EXECUTION SUMMARY

| Niche | Hook Angle | Magnet | Analogy | CTA |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|-----|
| Commercial Roofing | Invisible damage → visible loss | 72-Hour Storm Window Report | Grocery sampling | "Tap here — your window closes in 18 hours" |
| Commercial Solar | Neighbor comparison → fairness gap | Kilowatt Neighbor Report | Airline overbook | "Your neighbors are paying less. Want their deal?" |
| Debt Relief | Avoidance behavior → quantified pain | 90-Second Creditor Scorecard | Grocery checkout | "11 years vs. 3 years. Want to see how?" |

---

**Core rule across all three:** Never ask for a sale. Ask for a curiosity click. The magnet does the selling. The pitch closes the framework deal, not the client. We don't sell roofs, solar, or debt relief. We sell the person who WANTS the roof, the solar, or the debt relief. That's the product.
